🦁 Nyakinyua Premier League – Frequently Asked Questions

🎮 eFootball | Play With Passion. Fight for the Crown.

🎯 How is the League Structured?

  • Game Duration: Each match lasts 12 minutes
  • Entry Fee: Ksh 50 per participant

💰 What’s the Prize Breakdown?

Top 3 participants share the prize pool:

  • 🥇 1st Place – 50%
  • 🥈 2nd Place – 30%
  • 🥉 3rd Place – 20% (via playoff)

How does the 3rd Place Playoff work?

  • Players ranked 3rd to 6th play in a knockout mini-tournament
  • The winner takes home the 20% prize

📜 How are Matches Played?

Match Format:

  • 12-minute matches
  • No extra time or penalties
  • Fair play is expected at all times

What if someone gets disconnected?

  • If it happens before the 70th minute:
    • Resume the match from the point of disconnection
    • Score remains as it was
  • If a player refuses to continue, see the next point

What if someone quits the match?

  • If a player quits after the 70th minute while losing:
    • The match is considered final
    • Winner’s score is recorded
    • The quitter’s goals are void
  • No rematches, no messages, no disputes

🚫 What Happens if Someone Acts Unfairly?

  • Report any disputes or issues in the official group
  • Not ready to play? Reschedule before kickoff
  • No excuses once the game starts
  • Poor behavior or fake lagging may lead to a 0–3 loss

📅 What if Someone Misses a Game?

  • All games should be completed as per schedule
  • Missed game? It results in a 3–0 walkover
  • Repeated no-shows = disqualification
  • Grace Period: You have until 7 PM the next day to play. After that, a walkover is enforced.
“Don’t just play — show up with hunger. Win with pride, lose with honor. No complaints, no shortcuts, be at your Best.”
